## 👋 About us

Most operational loss stays invisible because every system involved is working correctly.

That's why we don't start an engagement by writing software. We start by measuring. Before we build anything, we work out what is leaking, what it costs in your own currency, and whether a system would fix it. Some of what we find turns out to be a process problem, and selling software for a process problem is how buyers end up paying twice.

## 🧭 Our principles

| | Principle | What it means |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| **01** | **Evidence before build** | Every recommendation traces back to records in your own systems. If we can't show you the underlying rows, we don't make the claim. |
| **02** | **You own what we build** | You own the code, data, and infrastructure. There's no proprietary runtime, no per-seat fee on your own operations, and no exit penalty. |
| **03** | **Measured, not asserted** | We agree a baseline in writing before the work starts, so the result at the end is a measurement and not a story. |

## 🛠️ What we do

### I. Business Health Check
*Find out what is leaking and what it costs, in your own numbers. We start with a time-boxed diagnostic, then leave behind a detection layer that keeps doing the job after we're gone.*

- **Business Health Check**: a structured examination across eight loss domains that produces an evidenced findings register.
- **Operational Intelligence Systems**: continuous monitoring that raises alerts as problems form. Each alert comes with evidence, an owner, and a deadline.

### II. Workflow Automation
*Take repetitive, cross-system work off people and hand it to systems and agents that can be audited, corrected, and held to a deadline.*

- **Business Process Automation**: the cross-system processes every operation runs daily, such as order to cash, procure to pay, approvals, and document handling.
- **AI Agent Development**: agents that carry out multi-step work end to end, with guardrails and approval gates.
- **WhatsApp & Communication Automation**: structured, logged follow-up with consent handled, on the channel people actually answer.

### III. Digital Foundation
*Build the record layer: the systems that capture the data everything else depends on. The other two verticals can't do better than the data this one captures.*

- **Custom Software, CRM & Portals**: internal systems and portals designed around the workflow, with audit trails and APIs from day one.
- **Mobile Applications**: data captured where and when things happen. Built for field teams, works offline, with evidence attached at the source.
- **Growth & Analytics Infrastructure**: instrumentation that ties spend to revenue, not to traffic.

## 🔁 How we work

> **Diagnose first. Fix the records if they need fixing. Automate last.**

```
  ┌──────────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────────┐
  │  1. ALWAYS FIRST         │ ──▶ │  2. THEN, IF NEEDED      │ ──▶ │  3. THEN                 │
  │  Business Health Check   │     │  Digital Foundation      │     │  Workflow Automation     │
  └──────────────────────────┘     └──────────────────────────┘     └──────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Business Health Check (always first).** Nothing else can be priced honestly until the loss is measured. A diagnostic can also conclude that no build is needed, and that's a valid result.
2. **Digital Foundation (then, if needed).** If the diagnostic finds that the underlying records are unreliable or missing, we fix the foundation before automating anything on top of it.
3. **Workflow Automation (then).** We automate only once the process is understood and the records can be trusted. Automating any earlier just makes an existing problem happen faster.

> [!NOTE]
> **The honest version:** a business that buys Workflow Automation without a Digital Foundation in place is paying us to automate against data that isn't captured reliably. We'll tell you that at the diagnostic stage, not at go-live.
